Privacy Policy

How we handle information and protect user privacy on the TrendShow platform.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because TrendShow is purely a discovery and curation portal, we do not require account registration, do not maintain customer profiles, and do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, payment details, or billing addresses.

2. Information We May Collect

When you interact with our website, standard non-identifiable web server logs may record your browser type, device screen dimensions, referring URL, and search queries within the search bar to help us improve user experience and interface responsiveness.

3. Cookies & Affiliate Tracking

Outbound merchant links may place standard cookies on your device via affiliate networks (such as Amazon Associates, Flipkart Affiliate, or relevant third-party affiliate programs). These cookies simply identify TrendShow as the referral source so that merchant commissions are correctly attributed.

4. Third-Party Websites

TrendShow contains outbound links to external retailers. Once you leave our domain, your activity is governed by the destination website's individual privacy policy and terms of service. We encourage you to review their policies upon arrival.

5. Policy Updates & Inquiries

We may revise this policy periodically to reflect operational enhancements. If you have any privacy questions, please contact us via our Contact Page.
